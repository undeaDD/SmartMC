---
title: "Expanded version support"
order: 4
---

Fabric and NeoForge already build on Minecraft 1.21.1, with Quilt covered for free via its built-in Fabric compatibility — their shared floor is settled at 1.20.2 (NeoForge's own earliest release), with the actual build target still to expand down to it. Classic Forge is back too, covering the legacy 1.19.2–1.20.1 range those loaders can't reach — modern-version Forge still isn't targeted, since NeoForge already covers that range and Forge's modern tooling is a separate, incompatible toolchain from the one used here.
