---
title: "Expanded version support"
order: 4
---

Fabric and NeoForge already build on Minecraft 1.21.1, with Quilt covered for free via its built-in Fabric compatibility. Version coverage expands down toward a 1.20.2 floor, possibly different per loader where needed. Classic Forge isn't targeted — its modern-version tooling is a separate, incompatible toolchain from the one used here, and Quilt already fills that third-loader role.
